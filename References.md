
## Psychometric g-factor

Human intelligence is intercorrelated
- Waterhouse, L. (2023). Why multiple intelligences theory is a neuromyth. _Frontiers in psychology_, _14_, 1217288.

Example of breaking down test scores into general factors
- Pokropek, A., Marks, G. N., & Borgonovi, F. (2022). How much do students’ scores in PISA reflect general intelligence and how much do they reflect specific abilities?. _Journal of Educational Psychology_, _114_(5), 1121.

Example of triangulating the g-factor from a set of tests
- Johnson, W., Bouchard Jr, T. J., Krueger, R. F., McGue, M., & Gottesman, I. I. (2004). Just one g: Consistent results from three test batteries. _Intelligence_, _32_(1), 95-107.
- Johnson, W., Te Nijenhuis, J., & Bouchard Jr, T. J. (2008). Still just 1 g: Consistent results from five test batteries. _Intelligence_, _36_(1), 81-95.

Reflective vs. Formative vs. Mutualist models
- Van der Maas, H. L., Kan, K. J., & Borsboom, D. (2014). Intelligence is what the intelligence test measures. Seriously. _Journal of Intelligence_, _2_(1), 12-15.

Unreliability of small-set g-factor extraction. Issues in traditional psychometric approach to extract g. LLM benchmarks at the hundreds are free of this problem (other problems are MNAR, etc.)
- Major, J. T., Johnson, W., & Bouchard Jr, T. J. (2011). The dependability of the general factor of intelligence: Why small, single-factor models do not adequately represent g. _Intelligence_, _39_(5), 418-433.

In humans, inspection time is correlated with general intelligence. The lesson is that we cannot rule out a benchmark like, say, ethical compliance as "non-intelligence" a priori.
- Kranzler, J. H., & Jensen, A. R. (1989). Inspection time and intelligence: A meta-analysis. _Intelligence_, _13_(4), 329-347.

Introduction to g
- Jensen, A. R. (2002). Psychometric g: Definition and substantiation. In _The general factor of intelligence_ (pp. 51-66). Psychology Press.

## Intelligence in AI/ML/Compsci

Operationalist/outcome-based conceptualizations of AGI
- Morris, M. R., Sohl-Dickstein, J., Fiedel, N., Warkentin, T., Dafoe, A., Faust, A., ... & Legg, S. (2023). Levels of AGI for Operationalizing Progress on the Path to AGI. _arXiv preprint arXiv:2311.02462_.

Fluid intelligence conceptualized as important for domain-general ability. But an issue is imposing an assumption that LLMs' intelligence are organized in a certain way a priori. Our results actually show that reasoning-related benchmarks are substantially non-general.
- Chollet, F., Knoop, M., Kamradt, G., Landers, B., & Pinkard, H. (2025). Arc-agi-2: A new challenge for frontier ai reasoning systems. _arXiv preprint arXiv:2505.11831_.
